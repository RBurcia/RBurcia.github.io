Automated Tensile Tester

Control and analysis software for a tensile testing rig. The app drives the stepper motor through load cycles, captures camera frames of the sample, and measures deformation from the images.

  • Camera-based image processing to track sample width and strain
  • Stress–strain and force–strain graphs with a Young's modulus calculation
  • Desktop UI with test caching and Excel export
